# Trademark And Branding Guidelines

The Trench.Tools name, logo, domain, visual identity, product names, and related
branding are trademarks or trade dress of the Trench.Tools project. They are not
licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License.

## Permitted Use

You may use the Trench.Tools name to truthfully describe the origin of the
software, link to the official project, discuss compatibility, or identify that
your work is based on Trench.Tools.

Examples of permitted use:

- "Forked from Trench.Tools"
- "Compatible with Trench.Tools"
- "Uses Trench.Tools under the AGPLv3"
- "Based on Trench.Tools, with local modifications"

## Restricted Use

You may not use Trench.Tools branding in a way that suggests your fork,
modified version, commercial service, hosted service, extension package, website,
or unrelated product is official, endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with
Trench.Tools unless you have written permission.

You may not:

- publish a modified version under the Trench.Tools name without clearly marking
  it as modified
- use the Trench.Tools logo or visual identity for a fork, hosted service, or
  commercial product without written permission
- register domains, social handles, package names, app listings, or communities
  that imply official Trench.Tools ownership or endorsement
- remove or obscure notices that identify the official Trench.Tools project

## Forks And Modified Versions

Forks and modified versions should use a distinct name and branding. Any public
distribution or hosted version should make clear that it is not the official
Trench.Tools project unless written permission has been granted.

## Contact

For permission to use Trench.Tools branding beyond these guidelines, contact the
project maintainers through the official repository or website.
